Camera + depth

The camera captures a visual scene. Time-of-flight (ToF) depth sensing adds distance, helping separate nearby objects from the background.

02
⌖

IMU + proximity

Gyroscope and motion sensors track RMIB’s movement. Proximity sensors provide another check near beds, people, and equipment.

Room, made navigable

RMIB combines sensor clues into a room layout: where objects are, where people may be moving, and which paths look clear.
